Frequently asked questions

Can I have a lost item delivered to me?

Yes. Report the item from your trip history, and your driver can return it to you for a small delivery fee.

Can I rent a car using CityPulse?

Rentals are available in select cities. Open Travel in the app to see what’s near you.

Can I request a ride that picks up friends in different locations?

Yes. Add up to three stops, and each rider gets a pickup point on the same trip.

Can I request a taxi on CityPulse?

In many cities you can book licensed taxis next to regular cabs, autos, and motos.

Is there a CityPulse ride option for 5 people?

Choose XL when you see prices. It seats up to six riders with luggage.
